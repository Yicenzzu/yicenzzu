## Aerosol-cloud interactions and atmospheric chemistry

Atmospheric aerosols influence clouds and air quality. My work examines gas-particle chemistry and heterogeneous and multiphase reactions through atmospheric modeling.

## Computational simulation of aerosol composition and multiphase chemistry

Using the particle-resolved [PartMC model](https://github.com/compdyn/partmc), I study how aerosol mixing state and other representation choices affect chemical processes and model predictions.

## Data science and high-performance computing

I develop analysis workflows for large scientific datasets, evaluate model predictions against observations, and run parallel simulations to quantify the effects of simplified aerosol representations.

## Scientific visualization

I use visualization to make complex aerosol composition and simulation results easier to inspect and communicate.
